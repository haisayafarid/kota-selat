📖 Harbour Master Codex

1. Spatial Divisions: The western channel (Col 0) is reserved for foreign fleets and the Port at [2, 0]. Watchtowers must guard the sandy Coast (Col 1). Inland tiles (Cols 2–4) host storage, dwellings, and civic administration.

2. Life Restoration (HP): Gaining a new Reputation Tier restores +1 City Health automatically. The Balai Penghulu at [2, 2] also offers emergency repairs for 15 Gold and 1 Labor.

3. The Bribe Protocol: If raiders outgun your defense, you can pay emergency tribute to buy safe passage and protect your port HP at the cost of your treasury and prestige.

4. Istana Melaka & Second Port: Upgrade the Balai Penghulu to the Istana Melaka (45 Gold, 8 Pop, Tier 3 Rep). This opens the Second Port at [4, 0]—doubling arrivals and tariffs!

5. Catastrophes & Outlaws: Beware of Cholera outbreaks reducing population, monsoon floods submerging coastal tiles, and outlaws stealing gold if unemployment is neglected!

6. Ultimate Victory: Reach Tier 5 Reputation (30★) with the Istana Melaka active to summon the Imperial Ming Treasure Fleet and claim sovereign victory!
