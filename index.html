🗺️ 24-Season Campaign Roadmap

The Sultanate of Melaka demands steady governance across 3 distinct historical chapters leading up to the Imperial Ming visit:

Babak I: Muara Berbatu (Seasons 1–8)

Establish trade storage, secure the coastline against raider skiffs, and survive Panglima Hitam's early probe at Season 8.

Babak II: Zaman Keemasan (Seasons 9–16)

Experience population booms and weather crises (Monsoon tides & Cholera). Upgrade to Istana Melaka to open the 2nd Port!

Babak III: Armada Laksamana (Seasons 17–24)

Withstand coordinated double-channel raids. Survive until Season 24 to welcome the Imperial Ming Treasure Fleet for complete victory.

📖 Harbour Master Codex

• Day Phase: Build, draft, and upgrade structures. Click Open Harbor when ready to bring in incoming ships.

• Night Phase: Merchant fleets dock to unload spices and pay tariffs. Defenses engage shadowing pirates.

• Stacking: Drag duplicate cards onto matching buildings to stack them up to ★3 for doubled benefits.

• Workforce Balance: Watchtowers and Warehouses require active labor. Keep your Fisherman Villages staffed!

⚜ KOTA SELAT: SYAHBANDAR 1500 AD

Prior to the Portuguese conquest of 1511, Melaka stands as the richest maritime entrepôt in the world. As Syahbandar, you hold sovereign responsibility over the harbor.

Your Administrative Mandate:

• Manage the Tides: Build during the Day (☀️); welcome fleets & repel pirates at Night (🌙).

• Balance the Realm: Match workforce to defense towers & warehouse storage to incoming cargo.

• Survive 24 Seasons: Advance through 3 Historical Acts and welcome the Imperial Ming Treasure Fleet.
